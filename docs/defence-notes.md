## 3. Methods

**Q1. Why a 3-seller game, T=40, five price levels?**
A. Stated as the design in `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-25-aptg-rl.md` (3 agents, actions {0..4}, coalition {0,1}, outsider 2, T=40 scripted) and in the paper's Methods. Why these particular values rather than others: not established; no sensitivity analysis over T or the adherence probability 0.8 exists in the repo (listed as future work: "longer episodes and denser windows").

**Q2. The mechanisms are your own scripts; is the success of the union circular?**
A. Partly, and the paper says so: "design (linear model, lags 0--2) we chose knowing the mechanisms" (Methods, Conditions) and "the three new mechanisms were written knowing which statistics cover which dependence types" (Discussion; also `FOLLOWUP2_PREREG.md`, Data). The hold-out of alternating and hidden-channel constrains only B9 and the RL investigator. Evidence is therefore about coverage in this simulator, not unseen coordination. Mechanisms written by someone else: not tested.

**Q3. How were thresholds set, and could they leak test data?**
A. All thresholds are fixed on validation negatives at 5% FPR and applied once to test/OOD (Methods; `PREREG.md` "Primary outcomes and operating point"). Splits are by whole episode with disjoint seeds (train 0-9, validation 50-61, test 75-94, OOD 100-109; confirmation 200-274). Realised FPRs still differ across detectors at the shared target (B1 0.067, B3 0.043, APTG 0.037, B8 0.020; `DEVIATIONS.md` 11), so TPR comparisons favour higher-FPR detectors; AUROC is unaffected.

**Q4. Why do the seeds, permutations and windows differ from the plan?**
A. `DEVIATIONS.md` 1-4: Westfall-Young maxT replaces BH because with at most 200 permutations BH over 6 pairs cannot reject; windows 20/stride 10; 30 permutations; 10/12/20/10 seeds per cell instead of ~100, for compute. These were decided from training-seed smoke tests (`scripts/smoke_window.py`) before test evaluation, as logged.

## 4. Results, study 1 (pre-registered)

**Q1. B3 beat B2 in AUROC, so why is H1 "not supported"?**
A. The rule in `PREREG.md` required the directional method to beat both B2 and B1 on TPR with a paired-bootstrap CI excluding 0. It beats B2 but B1 has higher TPR (dTPR B3-B1 -0.162 [-0.267, -0.060], `DEVIATIONS.md` 14). The verdict was changed to "not supported" after external review (commit `2fbdfc2`); the pre-review wording is in earlier commits, which a judge may ask about. The change is logged and no earlier result changed.

**Q2. What would falsify the "mechanisms are covered differently" finding?**
A. A detector that covers all four study-1 mechanisms at the validation threshold. Table 2 (`paper/tables/mech.tex`) shows none does: B1 is blind to complementary pricing, B2 is the reverse, B3 misses the hidden channel (0.10). Whether this holds under other noise levels or game lengths: not established.

**Q3. Why is H5 not supported even though APTG's clean-to-OOD drop is small?**
A. The rule needed a smaller drop than B1, B2 and B5 on both contrasts; APTG degrades more than B1 from seen to held-out mechanisms (0.35 vs -0.38). The contrasts were computed post hoc (`experiments/h5_bootstrap.py`, `DEVIATIONS.md` 14), not in the original scoring stage.

**Q4. Is H2 (prompt conditioning, the motivating idea) tested?**
A. No. `src/aptg/calibration.py` exists and is unit-tested but is not wired into the reported detectors; prompt covariates are constant within an episode (`DEVIATIONS.md` 8; Limitations 3). The no-false-alarm evidence under unilateral perturbation (Table 5, `paper/tables/neg.tex`) is the only related evidence, from 60 episodes per cell.

**Q5. Why did the graph score and the RL investigator not help?**
A. Only three windows per T=40 episode and 30 permutations (Limitations 2, 4, 5; `DEVIATIONS.md` 2-3); B9 on the same features reaches higher AUROC than APTG, so the features carry signal the hand-built score discards. Whether more windows or longer episodes would change this: not established.

## 5. Union and confirmation

**Q1. Why max-z and not a learned combiner or a Bonferroni-style OR rule?**
A. Max-z of robust z-scores was the simplest combiner pre-specified in `FOLLOWUP_PREREG.md`. No comparison with a learned combiner or a Bonferroni OR rule is committed: not established (the paper does not discuss it; expect it as a criticism).

**Q2. Was the exploratory union specified before it was run?**
A. `FOLLOWUP_PREREG.md` says it was written before the union was computed, but it was committed together with the results, so the order is not verifiable from git (`DEVIATIONS.md` 15, paper Appendix deviation 8). That is why the confirmation study exists.

**Q3. Why should the confirmation be trusted?**
A. `FOLLOWUP2_PREREG.md` was committed as `77ad20e` (authored 2026-10-30 22:56 +05:30) before the confirmation episodes were generated; seeds 200-274 are new; detectors, z references and thresholds are frozen from study 1; run once (`experiments/gen_confirm.py`, `run_confirm.py`; `DEVIATIONS.md` 16-17). Limits: H6-H9 were written after study 1's results were known; the new mechanisms were chosen knowing the detectors' coverage; same simulator.

**Q4. What would falsify the union result?**
A. Under the pre-registered rules of `FOLLOWUP2_PREREG.md`: H6 fails if U3's TPR advantage over B1 or the best single statistic had a CI including 0; H7 if any of the seven mechanisms had U3 TPR below 0.50 (minimum observed 0.60, threshold trigger); H8 if realised FPR exceeded 0.10 (observed 0.056); H9 if the held-out-prompt drop exceeded 0.10 (observed 0.054). A failure on learned or independently written collusion would limit the claim but is not covered by these rules and has not been run: not established.

**Q5. U3 AUROC fell from 0.990 to 0.965 and B1 from 0.850 to 0.756; is that consistent?**
A. The paper attributes B1's fall to three of the seven confirmation mechanisms lying outside lag-1 coverage and notes that B3 beats B1 on the new data, unlike H1, so "single-statistic rankings depend on the mechanism mix" (Results, H6). Numbers: `results/confirm/summary.json`, `paper/tables/confirm.tex`.

# COLDER: no single dependence statistic detects collusion

**No Single Dependence Statistic Detects Collusion: A Pre-Registered Study and a Confirmed Union Detector**
Aravinth K, Madras Institute of Technology, Anna University (aravinth3007k@gmail.com). With Apart Research.
Paper: [`paper/main.pdf`](paper/main.pdf) (LaTeX source in `paper/`). Licence: MIT (`LICENSE`). Cite via `CITATION.cff`.

The work began as a submission to the Apart Research AI Collusion Research Sprint (23-25 Oct 2026, Track 2, Detection and Audit of Collusion) and tested the
author's APTG-RL design (the proposal title is "Adversarially Perturbed Temporal Graphs for RL-Based Detection of Multi-Agent LLM Collusion"). Work after the
sprint carries the commit prefix `[post-sprint]` (see `DEVIATIONS.md`, entry 17).

## What the paper claims
A controlled, pre-registered study in a 3-seller repeated pricing game (T=40; scripted collusion mechanisms, benign and shared-model negatives, seven
conditions C0-C6, ten detectors, validation-fixed thresholds). Numbers, CIs and caveats are in the paper; they are generated by `scripts/make_report_assets.py`.
* **Pre-registered nulls.** H1 (directional predictive gain beats symmetric MI and net influence) is **not supported**; H5 (robustness) is **not supported**;
  H3 (temporal graph features) is not supported; H4 (RL investigator) added no value; H2 (prompt conditioning) is inconclusive because it was never tested.
* **Mechanism specificity.** Each dependence statistic covers a different set of mechanisms (net influence misses same-round complementary pricing, symmetric MI misses
  lagged copying, the parametric gain misses a parity-coded channel).
* **Union detector.** A max-z union of net influence, symmetric MI and directional gain (U3), found exploratorily, was confirmed in a second pre-specified study
  (`FOLLOWUP2_PREREG.md`, commit `77ad20e`) on fresh seeds and three new mechanisms with frozen thresholds: H6-H9 supported; AUROC 0.965, TPR 0.877 against 0.617 for
  the best single statistic, FPR 0.056; the weak spots are the lag-2 follower (0.74) and the threshold trigger (0.60).

## What it does not claim
No claim about real deployed agents, learned (rather than scripted) collusion, other games or intent; the scripted mechanisms are the author's own and the
union's coverage is partly by construction (the confirmation mechanisms were written knowing which statistic covers which dependence). The prompt-conditioned
baseline is implemented but not wired into the reported detectors. No priority claim for mutual-information or transfer-entropy detection. The Haiku pilot is descriptive only.

## Honest limits (full list in the paper)
The prompt-conditioned baseline (`src/aptg/calibration.py`) is implemented and unit-tested but is **not wired into the reported detectors**, so the
prompt-conditioning hypothesis (H2) is not tested and the out-of-calibration gate never fires (found in the final code review).
Simulator-only; scripted mechanisms are the author's construction; fewer seeds than planned (10/12/20/10 per cell); 30 permutations per edge;
max-statistic correction instead of BH (see `DEVIATIONS.md`); prompt-covariate ablation is vacuous for per-episode estimation; the Haiku pilot is
6 episodes per condition and its agents share subagent contexts. No claims about real deployed agents or about intent.

Dual-use note: the hidden-channel mechanism is a closed toy parity code; see Appendix A of the paper.
